01
The problem
Reporting lag
3–7 days from KPI breach to leadership visibility

Correction windows close before intervention is possible. Downstream financial and compliance exposure accumulates unchecked.

Analytical bottleneck
Up to 30% of senior PM capacity lost weekly

Analyst capacity consumed by data gathering across time zones rather than solution design. Briefing quality inconsistent and time-dependent.

Governance risk
Structured KPI data and incident logs never cross-referenced

Corrective action plans rely on memory and institutional knowledge. Root cause analysis is anecdotal, incomplete, and variable in quality.

02
What I built
Project Aegis replaces manual reporting with an autonomous AI governance agent. The moment a KPI breaches its SLA threshold, the system detects it, cross-references the corresponding operational logs, and generates tailored prescriptive briefings for leadership and operations teams — without human intervention.
Capability As-is With Project Aegis
Time to insight 1–7 days Minutes
Briefing preparation Manual — senior PM drafts from ad hoc data pulls Autonomous — AI-generated from operational data
Root cause analysis Retrospective; structured and unstructured data reviewed separately KPI data and incident logs cross-referenced simultaneously at point of breach
Governance scope One market at a time Pan-European — all markets monitored simultaneously
PM capacity freed Up to 30% weekly capacity on variance reports Reporting automated — PM redirected to corrective action
